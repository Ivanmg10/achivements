# V5 — What a visitor sees signed out (landing, auth, public pages)
Summary: The public screens split in two. The pages written as documents (rankings, `/compare`, `/track`, legal) already
speak in the site's own voice: condensed italic display titles, italic rank numbers, slanted podium steps, honest copy.
The first impression (landing and sign-in) does not. It is the most templated part of the site: the AI landing stack, a
generic hero line in plain extrabold, icon tiles, cursor glows, gradient washes, an underlined form heading, and no logo
anywhere (the friend-drawn logo is only the favicon). The art collage is the one thing on those screens that could only be
CheevoVault. The three things that most make these screens feel vibecoded:
1. **The landing template:** hero with a visual on the right, a 1+2 bento of icon tiles, numbered FAQ cards, a closing CTA
   card and a footer, with an accent glow behind most of them.
2. **The site's own voice is missing exactly where a visitor arrives first:** no display italic, no logo, no slant on any
   surface. The slant shows only on library buttons, next to hand-drawn straight ones.
3. **The same piece drawn twice:** a slanted library button next to a hand-drawn pill or rectangle (nav "Entrar", Steam,
   "Ver los rankings", "Ver más", the RA sign-up). Most of the "slanted feels strange" feeling on these screens comes from this.

Method note: no browser was used. The judgements come from the shots and the code. The 390 full-page shots are 375 px wide
(the capture's scrollbar), so their right 15 px are probably cut off. I do not flag a tight right gutter at 390 for that
reason. The Next.js dev "N" badge and the version tag are dev artefacts. antislop mode was not resolved (no saved
preference), so I applied it as an audit filter, per the brief.

## Slanted vs round in this area
The pairs: `landing__slanted` / `landing__round` (2560), `V5-login-form-slanted` / `V5-login-form-round` (crops),
`rankings-public__slanted` / `__round` (2560), `profile-teaser__slanted__2560` / `__round__390`. The repeated causes:

- **C1 · The slant lives only on library controls; surfaces never slant.**
  - Cards, frames, FAQ rows and the auth card are rounded rectangles of 20–32 px in both designs.
  - What leans in slanted: the primary and secondary buttons, the level tags, bars and podium steps.
  - So "slanted" is a soft round site with a few leaning parts, and those parts look misplaced instead of like a style
    (V5-07, V5-10, V5-17).
- **C2 · Many angles, not one.**
  - The tokens: `--slant-button` −12°, `--slant-pill` −14° (tags, badges), `--slant-mark` −20°, `--slant-line` −30°.
  - The `--clip-*` cuts are fixed px (3–14 px), so their angle depends on the element's height: ~34° on a 6 px bar, ~11°
    on a 100 px podium step.
  - Add the display italic's own lean, the collage tiles' ±4–11° in both directions, and the fan covers' ±14°.
  - Side by side (rankings: podium, badges, numerals; teaser: bar, badge, button) the leans visibly disagree.
- **C3 · Two slant styles.**
  - Buttons and tags are skewed **and** rounded (a soft rhombus: 12 px radius plus skew).
  - Bars and podium steps are sharp `clip-path` cuts with no radius.
  - The same gesture is drawn with a soft edge in one place and a hard edge in the next.
- **C4 · Hand-drawn pieces do not follow the design at all.**
  - The list: `LandingNav`'s "Entrar" pill, `SteamSignInButton` (16 px rectangle), the RA sign-up button, the teaser's
    "Ver los rankings", the rankings "Ver más" pill, the auth chips, the FAQ "+" circle, and the hero window's
    `rounded-[2rem]`.
  - In slanted they read as "forgot to lean". In round, the Steam rectangle reads as "forgot to round".
  - This is the most fixable cause: library pieces only (E-17, E-25).
- **C5 · Round has no character of its own here.**
  - On public screens, round differs only in buttons, fields and tags becoming pills. Surfaces go 20 → 24 px, which cannot
    be seen at 2560.
  - The type, the bars' ends and the podium change only as the tokens make them change.
  - It reads as "slanted minus the lean", the original round identity the owner misses, flattened.
  - The login form in round is the one place it is coherent: pill fields and a pill primary, except the Steam button.
- **C6 · Things that lean for other reasons compete with the slant.**
  - The collage tiles tilt left and right alternately; the fan tilts symmetrically.
  - Against a one-direction −12° language they add noise.
  - If the slant is the identity, the decorative tilts could lean the same way, or not at all.

## Cross-cutting patterns seen
1. **The same control in two looks** (C4): a slanted library button next to a hand-drawn straight one, on landing, login,
   register, teaser and rankings. Fixing E-17 and E-25 removes most of the "strange slant" on these screens.
2. **The display italic voice is used inconsistently:** rankings, SEO, legal and the guide have it; the landing, auth headings,
   the teaser figure and the FAQ title do not. Making every public title display italic is a few one-line changes.
3. **Accent glow washes in six places, each written differently:** the landing FAQ orb, the closing card, the hero vignette,
