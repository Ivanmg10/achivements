# V6 — The two designs (slanted, round) and the component library
Summary: the slanted design is not a design of slanted *shapes*: it is the round-cornered site with a slant applied to
its **smallest** pieces (22 px tags, 1 px hairlines, 4 px underlines, 6 px bar ends) and to one big one (the primary
button). Every large surface (cards, rows, hero, tiles, avatars, fields) stays a soft rounded rectangle, so the slant
reads as jitter on the edges rather than as a voice. The three causes that most make it feel "off": **(1) one slant,
about fifteen visible angles** (cuts are fixed pixels, so the angle depends on each element's height: 2.5° to 63° on
real screens); **(2) two slant languages that do not rhyme**: soft *skewed rounded* controls (lozenges) next to crisp
*clipped* bars with sharp corners; **(3) the same kind of element is slanted in one place and straight in the next**
(tags, controls, selected states) with no rule a reader can feel. Round, meanwhile, is "slanted with the cuts removed":
pills and capsules, but the italic display type, the layout rhythm and every device were designed for slanted, so it has
no signature of its own. The library is healthy at its core (Button, Field, Select, Switch, Modal) but thin at the
edges where the eye actually lands: tags, figures, bars, selected states, kickers.

**Capture caveat (important for every auditor):** the `app/` and `modals/` round shots are the slanted page. `racha`
round and slanted are byte-identical (same md5), and a pixel diff of every other `app/` and `modals/` pair at 2560 gives a
mean difference of 0.00–0.45 (the account's synced `shape-style` most likely overrode the capture's choice). Only
`public/` pairs (signed out, mean diff 0.03–5) and the catalogue's own side-by-side `Compare` panels truly show round.
This report takes round evidence only from those. See V6-17.

### V6-01 · HIGH · slanted
- **See:** `.audit/visual/crops/V6-cat-shape.jpg` (the `slantStyle(3 · 8 · 14)` specimen: three bars, three angles),
  `.audit/visual/crops/V6-podium-pair.jpg` (podium steps 1/2/3 lean differently), `.audit/visual/crops/V6-racha-bars.jpg`
  ("Cuándo juegas": the tall Monday bar leans less than the short Friday one), `.audit/visual/crops/V6-podium-390.jpg`.
- **What:** `--clip-3 … --clip-14` are horizontal offsets in **pixels**, so the visible angle is `atan(px / height)` and
  changes with every element's height. Measured from the code (heights from classes, angle from vertical):

  | Where | Cut / height | Angle |
  |---|---|---|
  | Button, header account plate, StatPill rule (`--slant-button`, skew) | — | 12° |
  | Segmented indicator, Switch, StreakTag, VaultLevelBadge, GameHeroChip, StatPill hover (`--slant-pill`) | — | 14° |
  | Podium steps desktop 1st / 2nd / 3rd (`slantStyle(14)` on 100 / 70 / 52 px) | 14 px | 8° / 11° / 15° |
  | Podium steps phone (72 / 48 / 36 px) | 14 px | 11° / 16° / 21° |
  | Following rankings teaser steps (`slantStyle(8)`, h-7) | 8 / 28 | 16° |
  | Best streaks bars (`StreakChart`, 22 px) | 6 / 22 | 15° |
  | Vault guide example bar (12 px) / table bar (8 px) | 5/12, 4/8 | 23° / 27° |
  | VaultPlatformSplit (8 px) | 3 / 8 | 21° |
  | ProgressBar `h-1.5` track ends / fill's leading edge (`--clip-3`, `--clip-fill`) | 3/6, 4/6 | 27° / 34° (two angles in one 6 px bar) |
  | ProgressBar `h-1` ends / fill edge | 3/4, 4/4 | 37° / 45° |
  | Heatmap range underline (3 px) | 3 / 3 | 45° |
  | "Cuándo juegas" bars (`StreakWeekdays`, 4–120 px by data) | 8 px | 4° … 63° |
  | Vault weeks chart (2–70 px by data), cover streak bars (≤24 px by data) | 3 px | 2.5° … 56° |
  | Tab / header nav underline (`--slant-line`, 4 px tall) | skew 30° | ≈2 px shift, reads straight |
  | Header search slash (`--slant-line`, 20 px tall) | skew 30° | 30°, clearly a "/" |
  | Accent marks (`--slant-mark`, skew-y on a 3–4 px bar) | 20° | ≈1 px, reads straight |
  | Streak day strip overflow cut (`--slant-cut`, skew-y) | — | 12° from horizontal |

  On `/racha` alone a reader meets 12°, 14°, 15°, 4–63° and 45° on one screen; on `/rankings` 8°, 11°, 15° on the three
  steps of one podium.
- **Why it matters:** a slant becomes identity only when it is *the same slant* everywhere; the eye reads a dozen
  angles as accident ("vibecoded": a value applied per component, never per system). This is the single most
  nameable cause of "it feels strange".
- **Code:** `src/app/globals.css:107-113, 253-259`, `src/components/ui/shape.ts` (`SlantPx = 3|4|5|6|8|14`),
  `ui/ProgressBar.tsx:43-44`, the eleven `slantStyle()` callers listed in "Why slanted feels off".
- **Proposal:** one angle. Replace the pixel cuts with a constant-angle cut: draw a bar's lean with
  `skew-x-(--slant)` on the bar inside a clipping parent (as Button already does), or compute the offset from the height
  the caller knows (`slantStyle(heightPx)` → `tan(--slant) × height`). Below a minimum height (≈16 px) draw no cut at all
  (see V6-03). `--clip-3…14` and `--clip-fill` go.

### V6-02 · HIGH · slanted
- **See:** `.audit/visual/crops/V6-cat-buttons.jpg` (slanted primary/secondary/danger: rounded parallelograms),

  `--r-badge`, `--r-track`, `--r-knob` → 2–4 px) so skewed controls are as crisp as the bars. Round is untouched (it keeps
  pills), and the two designs move further apart, which helps round too (see "Round's own character").

## Why slanted feels off

### Inventory of every slanted device

### The causes, named
1. **Angle drift** (V6-01): fixed-pixel cuts on variable heights. Fifteen angles; three on one podium.
2. **Soft vs crisp** (V6-02): skewed controls keep 6–12 px radii; clipped bars have none. Two slant languages.
3. **Slant on the smallest things** (V6-03): the large surfaces are all rounded rectangles; the slant only reaches tags,
   hairlines, underlines and bar ends, where it reads as jitter.
4. **No grammar** (V6-04): tags, controls and selected states are slanted in one place, straight in the next.
5. **Invisible slants** (V6-05): two tokens whose result is ≤2 px; code and catalogue pay for them.
6. **Slanted inside straight**: a slanted indicator in a straight rounded track (Segmented), slanted tags on straight
   rounded cards, a slanted badge clipped by its card (V6-08).
7. **Decorative slant on data** (V6-07): leaning vertical bars distort the comparison they exist for.
8. **Near-duplicate angles** (V6-06): 12° and 14° side by side in one row.

What already works and should be the model: the **primary button** (one big crisp gesture per screen), the **search
slash**, the **StatPill divider slash**, the **podium** (once its angle is constant), the **best-streak race bars**, and
the **overflow cut** (slant as a sign). Those are where the owner's liking of slanted comes from.

## Round's own character
- **Today it reads as slanted with the cuts removed.** Round was the original site; slanted grew past it (CLAUDE.md,
  #78) and #78 then made round "rounder", but by token substitution: every slanted device has a round fallback (lean → 0,
  cut → capsule, plate → pill) and none has a round *idea*. The podium pair shows it best: slanted steps are an image (a
  podium), round steps are three grey boxes (`.audit/visual/crops/V6-podium-pair.jpg`).
- **The lean survives in the type.** Every title, rank and big figure is `font-display italic` in both designs, so round
  still leans, just without anything answering it. On the login pair the italic-free "Inicia sesión" sits under a straight
  mark in both; on rankings the italic "1 2 3" sit on round slabs.
- **What round does have:** pills and circles for every one-line control (consistent since #78; the login pair's primary
  is a clean pill), round platform dots in the rankings filter (slanted shows squares), softer cards. Kept honestly, round
  is the calmer, friendlier design.
- **What would give it its own voice, within the current tokens:**
  1. **Upright display in round.** A token `--display-style: italic` (slanted) / `normal` (round), read by
     `.font-display` instead of the hard-coded `italic` class. Round's titles stand up; slanted's lean. The single
     largest difference a reader would feel, one token.
  2. **The dot as round's device** (the slash's counterpart): the tab marker a short capsule/dot centred under the
     label (`--tab-line-w`: full in slanted, 1.25rem in round), dots as kicker separators, the presence dot already there.
  3. **Thicker capsules**: progress `--bar-h` 6 px slanted / 8 px round; a capsule reads as "round" only when it is thick
     enough to show its ends.
  4. **A podium with round tops** in round (`rounded-t-card`, square base), so it is still a podium.

## Identity options
Three focused directions; none changes structure, all keep slanted the default. They combine; **A is the minimum**.

**A. One angle, used big (simplest).**
- *What changes:* `--slant` replaces five angle tokens; cuts follow it at any height; nothing under ~28 px slants
  (tags, hairlines stay as the one exception if B is taken, underlines, marks, 6 px bar ends straight); vertical data bars
  never lean. Slanted things per screen drop to 1–4, all the same angle, all large.
- *Tokens/library:* `--slant`; delete `--slant-pill/-cut/-mark`, `--clip-*`; `DataBar`; `Tag` straight; `slantStyle()`
  takes the height or disappears into `DataBar`. About −40 lines of tokens, −2 tokens per design.
- *Result:* the primary button, the segmented choice, the podium and race bars become the recognisable "CheevoVault
  lean"; the rest goes quiet.

**B. The slash as signature.**
- *What changes:* the search "/" and the StatPill divider are already the most "ours" slants. Make the slash the brand
  device: the current tab marked by a short slash before or under its label (instead of an invisible leaning underline),
  "/" as the separator in kickers and meta lines ("PS4 / 76 de 102 / 82 h" instead of "·"), the figure dividers kept.
  Paired with the logo (recolour only, never redrawn), it gives a mark people can recognise in a screenshot.
- *Tokens/library:* `--slash` (30°), `Slash` piece, Tabs indicator option, `Kicker` with a slash separator. Round draws
  the dot in its place (Round's own character, 2).

**C. Crisp slanted, soft round.**
- *What changes:* the slanted block gets small radii on controls and tags (2–4 px) and slightly tighter surfaces
  (`--r-card` 20 → 14 px, `--r-tile` 10 → 6 px), so slanted is angular and confident; round keeps pills and 24 px cards
  and takes upright display (`--display-style: normal`). Each design has a character, and the owner's feeling that
  slanted "damaged" round goes away because they stop sharing the same soft middle.
- *Tokens/library:* only the slanted and round token blocks plus `--display-style`; no component changes beyond
  `.font-display` reading the token. `designTokens.test.ts` still holds (round rounder).

Recommendation: A first (it removes most of the "off" feeling by deletion), then C's radii (a token-only change, easy to
try live on `/design` with the shape switch), then B if the owner wants a mark beyond the logo.

## Slanted vs round in this area
- Catalogue `Compare` panels: Button (lozenge vs pill), IconButton (rounded square vs circle), Switch (slanted rounded
  track vs pill), Segmented (slanted indicator in a straight track vs pill in pill), Pill (rounded rect vs pill), Tabs
  (identical at viewing size), DropSlot, Skeleton, ShowMore. Round is internally consistent; slanted mixes slanted and
  straight inside one piece (Segmented).
- Public pairs: login/register (`V6-login-pair.jpg`): one slanted button among straight fields and a straight Steam
  button vs one pill among pill fields and a straight Steam button. Rankings (`V6-podium-pair.jpg`): leaning steps at three
  angles vs plain slabs; slanted level tags vs pill tags; square vs round platform dots.
- Repeated causes: angle drift, soft-vs-crisp, slant on the smallest things, no grammar for tags/controls, invisible
  tokens; round lacks its own device and keeps the italic lean.
