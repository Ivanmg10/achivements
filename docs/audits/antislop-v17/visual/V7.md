## V7-02 · MED · vibecoded — colour discipline (R-29, R-13, R-01)

Too many hue systems compete on one screen:
- **The count.** I count about 24 hues: the accent, 4 platforms, 8 level tiers, 7 streak tiers, and
  hardcore/softcore/success/warning/danger/info. The home (`app/home-classic__slanted__dark__2560.jpg`, which is Venom)
  shows 8 hues besides the neutrals. Rankings shows the 4 platform colours, 2 tier colours, a violet streak, a green
  delta, a gold podium ring and green presence dots. In the header, the level chip (blue) and the streak chip (violet)
  sit side by side, and neither is the accent.
- **The same hue means many things:**
  - Yellow: hardcore, the 20–29 day streak, the #1 ring (literal `rgb(250 204 21)`, B-16), the favourite star and
    mastery.
  - Green: completed, Xbox, a positive delta, presence and the 10–19 day streak.
  - Violet: the default accent, the Expert tier and the 50+ streak.
- **Colours that collide exactly:**
  - Info equals softcore exactly in most themes.
  - In light-blue the accent equals softcore.
  - In black-ops-2 warning equals hardcore.
  - In dark-red, red, crimson and light-red, danger is nearly the same as warning.
- **Platform colours.** RetroAchievements is `bg-accent` and Xbox is `bg-success` (`PLATFORM_FILL`), so they change
  with the theme. In the blue themes the platform split bar shows three blues, and in Venom RA is white. Steam and PSN
  are hard-coded hex in 114 places (B-07).
- **Glows.** An accent glow follows the pointer (`.spotlight`) on 21 components. It adds to the finished-bar glow, a
  status glow and about 50 decorative gradients.

**Proposals:**
- Streaks in one hue, the accent, with intensity by tier.
- Level tier hues on the badge only.
- RetroAchievements gets a fixed colour in one `PLATFORM_HUE` map; the logo violet is a natural choice.
- Gold becomes a token.
- Drop `.spotlight`, so the finished bar keeps the only glow.
- A test that keeps the meaning colours at least about 30° apart in hue.
- CLAUDE.md contradicts itself on streaks ("in the accent" against "coloured by tier"). Settle that first.

## V7-03 · LOW · simplify **[cut/merge proposal]** — near-duplicate themes

Four pairs are near-duplicates:
- purple ≈ dark (accent 150 110 250 against 167 139 250),
- blue ≈ dark-blue,
- dark-white ≈ venom (both a white accent on near-black),
- the five light-* themes, which share one ground and differ only in the accent.

**Proposal:** cut to 13 themes: dark, light, red, green, blue, midnight, crimson, sakura, arctic, black-ops-2,
steam-retro, snake-eater, venom. Map each cut theme to its nearest kept theme, and count `users.theme` first.

## Side note (bug, MED)
When signed out, `ThemeContext` (`src/context/ThemeContext.tsx:30,43-53`) starts from `defaultTheme` and writes `dark`
over the stored theme. Public pages flip from the stored theme to dark, the stored theme is erased, and each fresh
sign-in flashes dark first. This is also why the signed-out theme shots are all dark.
