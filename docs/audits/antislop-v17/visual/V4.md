# V4 — Account page, dialogs, welcome

Summary: The account page works and its parts are clear. Its look is a generic settings dashboard, though: boxes inside
boxes inside cards, a coloured border on every platform card, a different colour on almost every figure, and five red
buttons on one screen. The dialogs are the least consistent area audited here: ten dialogs draw their head **five
different ways** (with or without ✕, hint, icon, and in two type sizes), and two place their footer in the body. The
welcome has the identity the owner likes, and it uses the slant **once** (its main button). The library then put the
slant on every control in the app, which is the most likely source of the "slanted feels strange" feeling.
The three things that make this area feel most vibecoded: (1) tiles inside tiles inside cards, with a colour per
figure; (2) dialog heads drawn by hand, each a little different; (3) danger red used as decoration (four "Desvincular"
slabs, "Cerrar sesión" and a whole "Eliminar cuenta" card).

> **Read this first (affects every auditor): the `round` shots of `app/` and `modals/` are not round.** They are slanted,
> and the theme is **Venom**, not dark. `usePreferencesSync` applies the account's own values on sign-in and the account
> wins (`shape-style` and `font-style` are synced keys, `src/utils/syncedPreferences.ts:8`; the theme comes from
> `users.theme`). Proof: `crops/V4-password-footer-round.jpg` shows slanted buttons, and `crops/V4-general-390-round-prefs.jpg`
> shows slanted switches. Several pairs are byte-identical (`user-psn`, `user-ra`, `user-xbox` at 2560). Only `welcome__round`
> is truly round, because the welcome sits outside `MainProviders`. Below, round is judged from the tokens
> (`globals.css:289-327`) and the welcome pair. To get real round shots, set the account's `shape-style` to `round`
> (Preferences → Layout) before taking them, or use an account with no synced preferences.

## Screen: Account page, General tab  (shots: `app/user-general__slanted__dark__2560.jpg`, `…__390.jpg`)
Identity: ours = the username in display italic over the avatar's own colour wash, the level bar with "#19 · Global",
the per-platform cards in the platforms' own words (Platino, Gamerscore, Maestrías). Generic = everything else: a form,
five boxed counters, a settings list, four identical connection cards. Keep the hero and the level row.

### V4-01 · HIGH · bug
- **See:** `app/user-general__slanted__dark__2560.jpg`, Preferencias → Tema / Idioma rows (`crops/V4-general-prefs-slanted.jpg`)
- **What:** The "Tema: —" and "Idioma: —" are real, but only for assistive tech. The screen shows "Venom" and "Español",
  but the row button's accessible name is `${label}: ${value || empty || '—'}`. Those two rows pass their value as
  `children` (swatch + name), not as `value`, so a screen reader hears "Tema: —, button" and "Idioma: —, button". It also
  fails WCAG 2.5.3 (label in name): the visible "Venom" is not in the name, so a voice user who says "click Venom" gets nothing.
- **Why it matters:** One of the two most used settings is announced as empty.
- **Code:** `src/components/user-page/profile-field/ProfileField.tsx:74` (aria-label), callers `user-preferences-general/UserPreferencesGeneral.tsx:58-67` (B-25 notes the `—` but not this).
- **Proposal:** Drop the `aria-label` and let the button's text content name it. Label and value are already inside the
  button; add the pencil/chevron as `aria-hidden`. If an explicit name is still wanted, add a `valueText` prop and pass
  `T.userTheme[name_…]` and the language label from the two callers.

### V4-02 · HIGH · a11y
- **See:** `crops/V4-general-prefs-slanted.jpg`, the four switches on the right
- **What:** On Venom, an "on" switch is a white slab with a white knob. The accent is `236 232 242` and the knob is `bg-white`,
  about 1.1:1, so the knob cannot be seen and the on/off state is not readable from the control. The line under the label
  does change ("Lo ve quien abra tu perfil"), so the state is in words, but the control itself is mute. Any light-accent
  theme ("Oscuro · Blanco") has the same problem.
- **Why:** WCAG 1.4.11 non-text contrast; antislop-human states.
- **Code:** `src/components/ui/Switch.tsx:34` (`bg-white` knob on `bg-accent` track).
- **Proposal:** Paint the knob with the same ink the primary button uses on the accent: `bg-bg-main` when `checked` (as
  `Button primary` uses `text-bg-main`), and white when off. One token, `--on-accent`, would also serve the selected
  language tile and the theme check.

## Slanted vs round in this area (re-checked 2026-11-06 with real pairs)
Real pairs: `app2/modal-{theme,layout,hidden-games,avatar,password,delete-account,admin-panel,search}__{slanted,round}__venom__{2560,390}.jpg` (clean),
and for the account page `app2/user-general__round__venom__{2560,390}.jpg` against `app/user-general__slanted__dark__{2560,390}.jpg` (Venom, earlier capture: the
points and ranking figures differ, ignore them). The welcome pair was already real. Crops: `crops/r-v4-*.jpg` (`z-` = enlargements, `r-v4-m-*` = dialog pairs).

**What really differs (all of it is control geometry; no layout moves):**
- **Buttons:** pills in round, lozenges in slanted: "Admin panel", "Cambiar contraseña", "Cerrar sesión" (`r-v4-ug-btns-s.jpg`), the four full-width
  "Desvincular …" slabs (`r-v4-ug-cards-s.jpg`), "Eliminar cuenta", and every dialog footer ("Cancelar" / "Guardar", "Unlock", "Eliminar mi cuenta":
  `r-v4-m-password-s.jpg`, `r-v4-m-delete-account-s.jpg`, `r-v4-m-admin-panel-s.jpg`).
- **Switches:** capsule track with a round knob vs a parallelogram track with a skewed knob (`r-v4-ug-prefs-s.jpg`).
- **Fields:** full capsules in round, rounded rectangles in slanted (`z-r-v4-ug-f2.jpg`); the description box keeps its radius in round with no resize grip, a grip
  in slanted (`z-r-v4-ug-ta.jpg`).
- **The Layout modal's selector:** the selected option is a capsule with a white ring in round and a ringed parallelogram in slanted (`r-v4-m-layout-s.jpg`, `r-v4-m-lay390.jpg`);
  its sample follows the shape (pill chip vs skewed chip, round vs cut bar ends).
- Header: search tick, level and streak chips, account plate; the preference row's theme swatch (circle vs square).

**What stays identical:** the whole account page layout (hero, level bar, tiles, preferences list, the four platform cards and their stat tiles), the theme
modal (every tile, the check, the headings: `r-v4-m-theme-s.jpg`), Hidden games, Avatar, the dialog frames, headings, field labels, the search modal
(`r-v4-m-search-s.jpg`: the rows, the icon tiles, the footer are indistinguishable) and the platform cards' coloured borders. At 390 the account page is the same except
the buttons and fields (`r-v4-m-ug.jpg`).

**Does round have a character of its own here?** Only the capsule: the buttons, switches, fields and the selector thumb are one consistent family and, on the
account page, round is coherent where slanted is the busiest screen of the app (four angles and many lozenges). That is the whole difference: **round is quieter, not
different.** The welcome pair (already real) stays the one place where round also changes the cards and number tiles.

**Corrections to the token-only reading above:**
- I feared "pill fields next to pill buttons next to pill switches, every control the same capsule". It **is** one capsule everywhere, but at 2560 it reads as calm
  and consistent, not as a template; the dense account page looks better in round than in slanted.
- I said the account page's four slanted unlink slabs and the dialog footers turn the accent into a band. In round they are full-width capsules and still
  band-like (`r-v4-ug-cards-s.jpg`): **the band is a layout problem, not a slant problem**; round does not cure it. V4-11 and V4-13 should be read that way.
- The 390 overlap of V4-03 ("Nv. 31 · Veterano" under "118.794 PV") is present in round too (`r-v4-m-ug.jpg`), so it is not design-related.
- The agreed causes hold: many angles on small things (hairline difference only visible in slanted), skewed-and-rounded controls (the lozenge buttons), full-width
  slabs. "Slant applied to every control rather than to the one primary act" is confirmed by the pairs: in the Layout modal the lean sits on a selected option that
  is not even a primary act.

## Cross-cutting patterns seen
1. **Dialog heads drawn by hand** (V4-11, E-02). This is the biggest single consistency fix in the area.
2. **Boxes in boxes, a colour per figure** (V4-05, V4-06). It drives the "dashboard template" feeling on the account page.
3. **Red as decoration** (V4-04). The destructive actions are the loudest elements.
4. **Slant on every control at four angles** (section above). It is the most likely cause of the owner's unease with slanted.
5. **Accent-on-accent contrast on light-accent themes** (V4-02). The switch knob, and probably every "white on accent"
   piece. A single `--on-accent` ink would fix the class.
6. **Shots for round and dark are not what they are labelled** (note at the top): the preference sync overrides them.
